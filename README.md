# 🇮🇳 Your Indian Translator v1

**YourIndianTranslatorv1** is an AI-powered translation model that converts English text into **colloquial Hindi**, specifically designed to help **female tourists** navigate India with ease.

---

## Problem Statement

Foreign travelers often face challenges due to **language barriers**, especially female tourists:

* ❌ Difficulty communicating with locals
* ❌ Misunderstandings causing frustration or missed opportunities
* ❌ Safety concerns when unable to express urgent needs

---

## Solution

An **AI-powered translation model** trained to provide accurate, context-aware translations for essential topics:

* ✅ Covers **130+ questions** across **13 critical topics**
* ✅ Topics include: menstrual tips, safety, pregnancy, medical emergencies, weather, internet connectivity, and more
* ✅ Designed with female travelers in mind, ensuring safe and smooth communication
